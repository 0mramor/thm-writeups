## What I learned

## Key concepts

**Key terms**
- **Scope** — the exact list of systems and actions allowed during a security assessment.
- **Vulnerability** — a hidden weakness in a system that an attacker could exploit.
- **Exploit** — a method or technique that takes advantage of a vulnerability.
- **Enumeration** — gathering information about a system, its users and services to find weak points.
- **Credentials** — login data (username/password) that grants access.
- **Authentication** — the step of verifying that someone/something really is who they claim to be when logging in.
- **Dictionary attack** — trying a predefined list of words to guess a password or username.

**Tools introduced**
- **Hydra** — brute-forces/guesses passwords using a wordlist.
- **Gobuster** — brute-forces/discovers hidden pages and directories on a web server using a wordlist.

**Career paths in cybersecurity**
- **Penetration Tester / Ethical Hacker** — safely researches vulnerabilities within a defined scope.
- **Vulnerability Researcher** — finds and confirms previously unknown weaknesses in software/hardware.
- **Red Team Operator** — simulates real adversary actions to test an organization's detection and response.
