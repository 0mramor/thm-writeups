## Key concepts

**Key terms**
- **Blue Team** — group of security specialists responsible for defending systems and responding to threats.
- **Client infrastructure** — the networks, servers, devices, and applications of an organization that need protecting.
- **Visibility** — the ability to see and monitor activity across different systems to spot potential problems.
- **Threat** — a potential danger (e.g. an attacker or malware) that could harm systems or data.
- **Prevention** — stopping threats before they can cause harm, by blocking, restricting, or reducing attack opportunities.
- **Detection** — the process of identifying threats or suspicious activity in networks and systems.
- **Mitigation** — actions taken to reduce or stop the impact of a threat once it's been identified.
- **Risk** — the likelihood and potential impact of a threat causing harm to an organization.

**Career paths in defensive security**
- **Security Operations Center (SOC)** — monitors networks and systems to detect and investigate suspicious activity or security alerts.
- **Threat Intelligence** — researches current threats, attackers, and trends to help organizations prepare for and defend against potential attacks.
- **Digital Forensics and Incident Response (DFIR)** — investigates security incidents to determine root cause and contain/recover affected systems.
